# Writing blog posts

Write for this publication (yurimutti.com), not a generic house style.

## Process

1. Read repository instructions ([AGENTS.md](../../../AGENTS.md)), the post
   frontmatter shape, and a couple of recent posts under `src/app/posts/`
   before drafting.
2. Define audience, reader problem, intended outcome, claim/evidence boundary,
   language, voice, post shape, and publication status.
3. Use [technical-blog-structure.md](references/technical-blog-structure.md) to
   select tutorial, conceptual, or opinion structure.
4. Use [voice-and-style.md](references/voice-and-style.md) to infer and preserve
   author voice without copying sentences from existing posts.
5. Read [nextjs-mdx.md](references/nextjs-mdx.md) for this repo's MDX conventions
   (frontmatter, `BlogPost` wrapper, headings, code, Mermaid).
6. Draft explicit, searchable titles and an introduction that opens with a
   thesis or concrete fact, not "why X matters nowadays" context-setting.
7. Prefer concrete examples, runnable commands, expected outcomes, trade-offs,
   limitations, primary sources, and honest uncertainty.
8. Before finishing, apply the writing-technical-edit skill to strip AI writing
   patterns, then validate with
   [quality-checklist.md](references/quality-checklist.md).

Do not invent lived experience, citations, measurements, credentials, quotes,
or publication authority. Drafting or editing does not authorize publishing.
